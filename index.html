12 questions before you choose your goals

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to the goals that will actually stick.

  1. When did I feel most rested in 2026, and why?
  2. What drained my energy most this year?
  3. How many hours do I really sleep on a normal night?
  4. Which habit do I keep promising myself and never start?
  5. Who leaves me feeling better after we meet?
  6. What do I say yes to that I want to say no to?
  7. What did I love doing as a child that I stopped?
  8. Where in my week is there time only for me?
  9. Which check-up or health task have I postponed?
  10. How do I talk to myself when something goes wrong?
  11. What would a perfect slow Sunday look like?
  12. If 2027 goes really well, how do I feel by next December?

Plan your self-care year in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: look back honestly. Note what gave you energy in 2026 and what took it.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below. Short, honest answers are enough; nobody else reads them.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three goals. Not ten. One for rest, one for your body or mind, one for joy or people.

Questions

What should go on a self care vision board?

Put the goal in your own words, a first step you can do this week, and why it matters to you. This page has 36 self-care goals in 6 groups (rest and sleep, body, mind, boundaries, joy, people), each with a first step, so you can pick three and make them visible.

Does a self care vision board actually work?

A board does not rest for you, but seeing the goal every day reminds you to choose it when the week gets busy. The habit that works best is putting it on your phone lock screen.

How many self care goals should I set for 2027?

Three is enough: one for rest, one for body or mind, one for joy or people. Fewer goals get kept more often.

What are good self care goals for busy people?

Small, daily and specific: a fixed bedtime, a 30-minute walk, no work messages after 7 pm. Start with one and add the next when it feels easy.
